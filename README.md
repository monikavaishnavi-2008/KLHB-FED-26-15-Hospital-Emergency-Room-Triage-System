Project Abstract:Abstract
The Hospital Emergency Room and Triage System is a healthcare management system designed to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of emergency department operations. The system focuses on the rapid assessment, prioritization, and management of patients based on the severity of their medical conditions. Upon arrival, patients are registered and their essential medical information, symptoms, vital signs, and medical history are recorded. Based on these factors, the triage process categorizes patients according to the urgency of treatment, ensuring that critical cases receive immediate medical attention.

The system provides healthcare staff with a centralized platform to monitor patient queues, assign patients to appropriate medical departments or doctors, and track their treatment status. It can also maintain electronic patient records, generate alerts for critical cases, and provide real-time information about available beds and emergency resources. By reducing manual paperwork and automating routine processes, the system helps minimize waiting times, improve resource utilization, and reduce the possibility of errors.

The proposed system aims to provide a faster, organized, and patient-centered emergency care process. It can support doctors, nurses, and hospital administrators in making timely decisions while improving overall emergency room management and quality of healthcare services.
